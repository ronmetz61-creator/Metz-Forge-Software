# Capacity Planner Project Vision
## Company
Metz Forge Company
## Product
Capacity Planner
## Purpose
Capacity Planner is a manufacturing scheduling and resource-planning application designed to help organizations understand project workload, availablecapacity, bottlenecks, constraints, and the effects of changing resources.
## Primary Goal
The application will allow users to compare project demand against available resources and identify where schedules may be delayed or overloaded .
## What the Application will do
- Store Project information
- Store available resources by department
- Calculate available capacity
- Compare workload with capacity
- Identify bottlenecks and constraints
- Display schedules and respource demand visually
- Allow authorized users to adjust staffing and capacity assumptions
- Support what-if scenarios
## What the Application Will NOT do
The application will not make management decisions automatically. It will provide accurate information to support decision-making.
## Initial Users
- Project Managers
- Department managers
- Operations leadership
- Resource planners
## Long-Term Vision
The core scheduling engine will be reusable across differenet manufacturing companies. Company branding, departments, resources, capabilities, and business rules will be configurable without rewriting the entire application.
